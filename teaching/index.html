Teaching content

The notebooks in this repository, organized by part. Nothing here is rewritten: the pages render the raw .ipynb files in the browser, keeping markdown, code and the saved outputs.

Topics and how they map to exercises

A notebook is matched to a topic when the topic name (or a word of it) appears in the notebook's title, path or markdown. Override any notebook's title, group or topics in data/curriculum.json, then re-run python3 tools/build_index.py.

Notebooks come straight from the repository and are displayed read-only.
