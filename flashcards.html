Where the cards come from

Cards live in data/flashcards.json — one entry per method. does is the explanation in plain words, call is the line to practice, and output is what it prints. assignment is the free-text label the deck is grouped under (use your assignment names), library is what the card is filed under when grouping by library, and topics are the ideas it belongs to. Add cards by editing that file; no rebuild is needed, only a reload.

Cards in this deck stay inside the methods the class teaches; replace or delete any seed card that goes beyond your syllabus. A card with "courses": ["fin400"] stays out of the other class; leave courses out to share a card with every class.

The cards themselves are stored in the repository. Your known / review marks are stored only in this browser (localStorage, key pypractice:flashcards), so they follow the browser and not the account — clearing site data or switching machines starts them again.
